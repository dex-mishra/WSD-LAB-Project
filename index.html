Industrial Engineering Case Study

Small-Scale Food Manufacturing
Digital Twin

Farm-to-consumer flow for a small food unit in India, focused on inventory and cold-chain resilience.

  • SOURCE Post-harvest losses across 54 commodities valued at roughly ₹1.53 lakh crore/year (NABCONS/MoFPI, 2020–22).
  • SOURCE Official baseline of 8,815 cold-storage facilities / 40.22 million MT capacity (as of 30 Jun 2025).
  • PROPOSED All demo loss rates, prices, and shelf-life values are illustrative and editable.
